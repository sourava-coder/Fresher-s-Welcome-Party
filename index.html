About the Event

Dear Student,

This gathering is organized to formally welcome our new students and mark the beginning of their journey on campus. The evening will include live music, student performances, light refreshments, and dedicated time for freshers and seniors to connect. It is an opportunity for new students to meet faculty, seniors, and peers in a relaxed, welcoming setting as they begin this new chapter. Your presence would be a valued addition to the occasion.
